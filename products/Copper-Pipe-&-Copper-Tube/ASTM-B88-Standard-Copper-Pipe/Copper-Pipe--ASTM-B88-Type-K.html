Copper Pipe - ASTM B88 Type K
Please Click to View Sizes Available
 
ASTM B88 TYPE K hard drawn copper pipe is suitable for underground hot and cold water piping, gas, sanitation, radiant heat piping and oil pipelines. Type K copper pipe is used where safety standards for oxygen and other positive-pressure medical gases are involved, in which case the pipe must be suitably cleaned and plugged. It is also used elsewhere in a wide range of engineering, steam and pipeline services.
 

TYPICAL CHEMICAL COMPOSITION
Cu: min 99.9%
P: 0.015-0.040%
