Copper Pipe EN 1057 TYPE X (Previously BS 2871 Table X)
 
 
EN 1057 Type X half hard temper copper pipes are generally used in above ground services including drinking water supply, hot and cold water systems, sanitation, central heating and other general purpose applications.
 

TYPICAL CHEMICAL COMPOSITION
Cu: min 99.9%
P: 0.015-0.040%
