Bronze Fittings and Flanges Supplier in Singapore

We supply bronze fittings and flanges in Singapore to ANSI B16.18 and ANSI B16.24, in BSP and NPT threads. The range includes bushings, caps, couplings, crosses, 45° and 90° elbows, street elbows, flanges (125#, 150#, 300#, PN16, 5K, 10K), lock nuts, nipples, plugs, reducers, tees, street tees and unions. Contact us with your type, size and thread for availability and a quote.
