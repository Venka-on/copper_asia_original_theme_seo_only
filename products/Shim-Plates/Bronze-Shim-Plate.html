Phosphor Bronze - Strips, Coils & Shim Plate (JIS H3100)

Phosphor bronze shim plate is most widely used in spring applications where its excellent resistance and fatigue endurance are major advantages. It has low coefficient of friction running against steel combine with high strength which make it ideal choice for wear resistance guide strips and similar bearing applications involving boundary lubrication conditions. Corrosion resistance of phosphor bronze is at least equal to copper but the tin addition gives advantages in salt water and similar aggressive moist conditions where it is superior to copper.
