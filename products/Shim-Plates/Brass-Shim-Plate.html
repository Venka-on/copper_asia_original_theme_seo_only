Brass Shim Plate (JIS H3100)

Our brass shim plate has great ductility and strength and is used where deep drawing qualities are required. It is used extensively for stamping, deep drawing, forming and polishing. Its distinctive clear bright yellow colour makes it suitable for ornamental and architectural applications.
