Shim Plates

Shim Plate Supplier in Singapore

We supply shim plates in Singapore in stainless steel 304 and 316L, copper, brass and bronze. Stainless steel 304 is a general-purpose choice, 316L resists pitting and crevice corrosion in harsher environments, copper offers high thermal and electrical conductivity, and brass and bronze machine well and resist wear. Tell us the material, thickness and size and we will confirm availability and send a quote.
