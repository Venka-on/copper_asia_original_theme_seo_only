Lead Wools 
 
Lead Wools is suitable as a filling material when welding lead as a joint filler, adjusting natural stone or as a radiation shielding material in lead aprons. When sealing joints with lead wools, use a pneumatic hammer.

 

Our lead fillings come in big bags of approximately 25kg.
