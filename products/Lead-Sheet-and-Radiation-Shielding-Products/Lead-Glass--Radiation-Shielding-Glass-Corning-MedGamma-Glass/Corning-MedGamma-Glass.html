Corning® Med-Gamma® Glass

Radiation Shielding Glass for medical, technical and research applications.
 

For more product information, please click the link:
Corning® Med-Gamma® Glass Product Catalogue
 

Corning® Med-Gamma® Glass is a high-quality, neutral appearance glass for protective shielding from Positron Emission Tomography (PET) gamma-based radiation at various keV levels. Its high quality and neutral appearance provide excellent visual clarity. It is supplied as polished plates with dimensions up to 2800 x 1400 mm.

Main Applications:

 • Occupational protection from PET gamma radiation used in scanning techniques for diagnostics and treatment.
 • Cyclotron maintenance.
