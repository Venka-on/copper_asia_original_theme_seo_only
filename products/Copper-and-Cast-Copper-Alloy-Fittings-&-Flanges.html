Copper and Cast Copper Alloy Fittings & Flanges

Copper and Cast Copper Alloy Fittings
Authorized Distributor of NIBCO® Fittings
 
Copper and cast copper alloy fittings are used extensively in residential, commercial and industrial applications worldwide. For example, copper fittings are used in plumbing systems to convey portable water in buildings & homes and in air conditioning and refrigeration systems.

Copper alloy fittings are used in plumbing systems for conveying many diverse fluids for the oil, chemical, process and marine industries.
 
Compliance Standards: 

               a) ANSI B16.22 or KS D5578
               b) ANSI B16.18
               c) AMSE B16.24
               d) BS EN 1254-2 : 1998
