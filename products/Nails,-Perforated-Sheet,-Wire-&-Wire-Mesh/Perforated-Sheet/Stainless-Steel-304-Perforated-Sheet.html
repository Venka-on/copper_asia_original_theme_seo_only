Stainless Steel 304 Perforated Sheet
 

Stainless Steel 304 Perforated Sheet is widely used in most industries for various purposes. It may be used as screens in separators, sunscreens, ventilation, walkways or as decorative designs. They provide the perfect solution for situations that require the passage of air, liquids, sound or even solid materials. Being versatile and having a modern touch by virtue of their geometric designs, perforated sheets have been much sought after in the engineering, architectural designing and food related industries. 

 


APPLICATIONS
 
Acoustic capabilities:

•  Acoustical insulation panels;

•  Sound muffler to reduce noise;

•  Speakers.

 

Architectural innovation:

•  Decorative Panels, Ceilings, Floors, Stairways, Shades, Grills and Dividers;

•  Vents, Light fixtures, Enclosures for animals;

 

Industrial needs:

•  Control flow rate of Air, Gas and liquid;

•  Filtration/sorting/sizing;

•  Oil and fuel filters;

•  Railways and Expressways fencing screens;

•  Cooling environment in heat generator/circuiting of Airconditioning/Ventilation

    system.
