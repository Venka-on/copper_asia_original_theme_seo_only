Copper Roves (Copper Washers)

Copper nails are traditionally used with matching copper roves to form a copper rivet. Copper roves are conical washers. A copper nail is hammered through the joint, a rove is pushed over the end (concave side inward) and the nail is peened over to form a copper rivet. The rove should be slightly under sized so that it can be driven tightly and securely onto the nail to prevent from falling off.
