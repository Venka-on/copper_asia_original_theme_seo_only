EDM Graphite - Grade S88


Send Enquiry   

Electrical Discharge Machining Graphite Grade S88

S88 is an ultrafine grain, high density, high strength isotropic EDM graphite. It offers outstanding wear resistance and very fine surface finish. Its high strength enables machining to less than 0.005 inches thick.

APPLICATIONS
  • Superfine detailed finishing electrodes
  • Excellent corner wear resistance
  • Machinable to less than 0.005 inches thick
  • Aerospace and Plastic Mould applications

EDM Graphite Grades are designed to span the range of application conditions from high metal removal rates to fine detail finishing.

TYPICAL PROPERTIES @ ROOM TEMPERATURE
  • Density: 1.88 g / cc
  • Specific Resistance:
- 15.5 µΩm
- 0.00073 Ω inches
  • Flexural Strength: 
- 12000 p s i (4 point test)
- 13500 (3 point test)
  • Hardness - 75 Shore
  • Average Grain Size - < 2 microns
  • Ash Content - 0.15%
  • Thermal Conductivity - 80 W / mK
  • Young´s Modulus - 1.9 x106 p s i
