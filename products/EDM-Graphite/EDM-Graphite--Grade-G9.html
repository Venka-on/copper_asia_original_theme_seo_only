EDM Graphite - Grade G9


Send Enquiry   

Electrical Discharge Machining Graphite Grade G9

G9 is a superfine grain, high density, high strength isotropic EDM graphite. The excellent properties of G9 enhances the finishing EDM process by providing good surface finish and wear resistance.

APPLICATIONS
  • Fine detailed finishing electrodes
  • Lower wear plus details
  • Plastic injection moulds
  • Diecast moulds
 
EDM Graphite Grades are designed to span the range of application conditions from high metal removal rates to fine detail finishing.
 
TYPICAL PROPERTIES @ ROOM TEMPERATURE
  • Density - 1.87 g / cc
  • Specific Resistance: 
- 11.8 µΩm
- 0.00046 Ω inches
  • Flexural Strength: 
- 8000 p s i (4 point test)
- 9300 (3 point test)
  • Hardness - 70 Shore
  • Average Grain Size - < 5 microns
  • Ash Content - 0.15%
  • Thermal Conductivity - 120 W / mK
  • Young´s Modulus - 1.7 x106 p s i
