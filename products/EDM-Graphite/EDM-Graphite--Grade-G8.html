EDM Graphite - Grade G8


Send Enquiry   

Electrical Discharge Machining Graphite Grade G8
 
G8 is a high strength fine grain isotropic EDM graphite. It is a mid range electrode which can be employed in both the roughing and finishing EDM process.
 

APPLICATIONS
  • High metal removal rates
  •   High strength, low wear mid range EDM electrodes
  • Plastic moulds
  • Diecast moulds
 
EDM Graphite Grades are designed to span the range of application conditions from high metal removal rates to fine detail finishing.

TYPICAL PROPERTIES @ ROOM TEMPERATURE
  • Density - 1.83 g / cm3
  • Porosity (Open) - 10 %
  • Grain Size - 10 µm
  • Pore Size - 1.5 µm
  • Permeability (air at 20°C) - 4 x 10-2cm2 / sec
  • Hardness Rockwell B - 95 (HR 5 / 100)
  • Bending strength - 60 N / mm2
  • Young´s modulus - 11.5 kN / mm2
  • Electrical resistance - 13 µΩm
  • Thermal conductivity - 100 Wm-1 k-1
  • Thermal expansion (20-200°C) - 4.0 x 10-6 / K
  • Ash value - 0.2 max.%
