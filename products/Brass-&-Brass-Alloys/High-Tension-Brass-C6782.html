High Tension Brass C6782 – Strength and Reliability

C6782 High Tension Brass

High Tension Brass C6782 is valued for its excellent performance and reliable operation.  It has high conductivity and durability and excellent corrosion resistance properties.  It is commonly used in marine hardware, valve seats, synchronizer ring, propeller shaft, pump shaft, fasteners, lead screw nuts & etc.  It is also used as EDM electrode, high-voltage discharge tube electrode, resistance welding electrode and electric spark electrode.
