Brass Sheet & Brass Plate


Send Enquiry   

Brass Plate C2680, C2720, C2801
 
Brass Sheet & Brass Plate are used extensively for stamping, deep drawing, forming, polishing and machining. Their distinctive clear bright yellow colour makes them suitable for ornamental and architectural purposes. They are highly essential material used in automotive, marine engineering, telecommunications, aerospace, oil & gas exploration and many other applications.
