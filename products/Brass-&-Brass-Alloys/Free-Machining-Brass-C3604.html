Free Machining Brass C3604

Durable and Efficient Brass C3604 for Machining

Free Machining Brass C3604
 
C3604 is a free machining brass which achieves maximum outputs on the highest speed automatics. This brass also enables the machine tools to have a longer lifespan. It is widely used in manufacturing hardware, connector, flange and other machine parts by lathe and CNC.
