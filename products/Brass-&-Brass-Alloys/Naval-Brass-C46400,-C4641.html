Naval Brass C46400, C4641

Naval Brass C46400 and C4641 Supplier in Singapore

Naval brass C46400 and C4641 is a copper-zinc alloy with a small tin addition that resists seawater corrosion, so it is widely used for marine hardware, shafts, fasteners and condenser plates. Teck Leong Industries supplies naval brass plates and rods in Singapore. Tell us the grade, form and size you need and we will confirm availability and send a quote.
