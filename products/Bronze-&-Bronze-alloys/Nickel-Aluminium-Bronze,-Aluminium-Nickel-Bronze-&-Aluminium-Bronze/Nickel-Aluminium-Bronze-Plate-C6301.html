Nickel Aluminium Bronze Plate C6301


Send Enquiry   

Aluminium Bronze Plate C6301 (Hot Rolled)

Aluminium Bronze Plate C6301 is a high strength and wear resistant alloy plate, possessing excellent corrosion resistance property.  It is an excellent choice for high strength applications involving corrosion, heavy loads, abrasive wear, abrasive wear resistance and friction,  The addition of nickel increases its alloy strength without diminishing its ductility, toughness and excellent corrosion resistance properties. 
 
APPLICATIONS
It can be suitably used in the demanding fields of aircraft, pressure vessels, condensers, heat exchangers, oil and gas exploration, tanks, structural members, gears, propellers, ship propellers.
