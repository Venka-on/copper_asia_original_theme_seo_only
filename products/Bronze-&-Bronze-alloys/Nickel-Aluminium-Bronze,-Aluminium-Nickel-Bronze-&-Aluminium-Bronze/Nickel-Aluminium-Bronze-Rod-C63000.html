Aluminium Nickel Bronze Rod C63000

Aluminium Nickel Bronze Rod C63000 is wear-resistant and high strength (both at room and elevated temperatures) alloy rod.  It is an excellent choice for bearing and bushing applications involving heavy loads, adhesive wear, friction, abrasive wear and corrosion. The addition of nickel increases the alloy strength without diminishing its excellent ductility, toughness and corrosion resistance properties. It also has good fabrication and welding properties.  It can be suitably used in the demanding fields of Aircraft, Oil & Gas Exploration, Earth Moving Equipment and Heavy Transportation.
