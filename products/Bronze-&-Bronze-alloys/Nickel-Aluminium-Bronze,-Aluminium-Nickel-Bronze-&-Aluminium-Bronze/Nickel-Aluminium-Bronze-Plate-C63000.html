Nickel Aluminium Bronze Plate C63000
(For Pressure Vessels, Condensers & Heat Exchangers)
 
Aluminium Nickel Bronze Plate C63000 is a high strength (both at room and elevated temperatures) and wear resistant alloy plate, possessing excellent corrosion resistance property. It is an excellent choice for high strength applications involving corrosion, heavy loads, abrasive wear, abrasive wear resistance and friction. The addition of nickel increases its alloy strength without diminishing its excellent ductility, toughness and excellent corrosion resistance properties.  It also has good fabrication and welding properties.  It can be suitably used in the demanding fields of aircraft, pressure vessels, condensers, heat exchangers, oil & gas exploration, tanks, structural members, gears, propellers, ship propellers.
