Aluminium Nickel Bronze Hollow Bar AB2, C95500 & C95810
 

Aluminium Nickel Bronze Hollow Bar AB2,  C95500 & C95810 have good corrosion resistance and good wear resistance properties and are very high strength with high resistance to oxidation. They possess very good strength at elevated temperatures. They are typically used in the chemical, marine and oil operation where chemical and sea water are prevalent. Common applications are valve fittings, propellers, propeller shafts, heavy duty bearings, landing gear parts, gears, worm wheels, non-sparking tools, etc.
