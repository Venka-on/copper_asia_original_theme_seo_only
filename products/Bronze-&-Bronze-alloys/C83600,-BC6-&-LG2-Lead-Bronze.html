C83600, BC6 & LG2 Lead Bronze

Lead Bronze C83600, BC6 & LG2
 
Lead Bronze C83600, BC6 & LG2 are commonly known as Lead Tin Bronze, Gunmetal or Lead Gunmetal. Lead is added to improve their machinability which increases, although not proportionately, with higher lead content. Lead bronze is a general purpose bronze which has the most versatile applications and is used in making hydraulic components, gears, worm wheels, value seats, slide valves, rock drill parts, crusher bearings, hose nozzles, rollers, washers, wearing rings, sleeves..
