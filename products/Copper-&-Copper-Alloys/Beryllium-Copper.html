Beryllium Copper C17200 and C17500 Supplier in Singapore

Beryllium copper combines high strength and hardness with good conductivity. C17200 is used for springs, precision instruments and tooling, and C17500 where good thermal and electrical conductivity is needed. Teck Leong Industries supplies beryllium copper in Singapore.
