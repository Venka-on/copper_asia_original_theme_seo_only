BERYLLIUM COPPER C17500
 
Beryllium Copper C17500 has high thermal conductivity combines with good hardness and high temperature strength. Both these properties together make it most suitable for such applications as heat dissipating modules, resistance welding components and also in other electrical applications. It also provides good resistance to tempering. It is not suitable for case hardening or nitriding.
 
APPLICATIONS 
  • Injection mould and plastic mould toolings, e.g. cavity inserts, manifolds, nozzles, etc.
  • Current carrying springs
  • Springs, electrical switches, diaphragms, bellows & connectors, relays, contacts, washers etc.
  • Resistance welding equipment components, e.g. shanks, holders, adaptors & related parts
  • Electrodes for projection welding & spot welding
  • Butt welding jaws
  • Contact tips for submerged-arc-welding
