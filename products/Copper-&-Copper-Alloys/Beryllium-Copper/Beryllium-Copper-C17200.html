Beryllium Copper C17200

Beryllium Copper C17200 is a precipitation hardened alloy with good thermal conductivity. It exhibits the highest strength and hardness in comparison with commercial copper alloys. It has the strength and hardness similar to that of steel with superb spring properties, good corrosion resistance, non-sparking, non-magnetic superior wear and anti-galling properties and high fatigue strength & toughness under harsh condition. Not suitable for case hardening or nitriding
 
APPLICATIONS  
  • Injection mould and plastic mould toolings, e.g. cavity inserts, manifolds, nozzles, etc.
  • Electrical & electronic connectors in computer, automotive electronic applications, 
    undersea fibre optic cable repeater housing, etc.
  • Current carrying springs
  • Critical components (e.g. drill collars, wear surfaces, bushings, bearings, etc.) 
    in Oil & Gas exploration
  • Landing gear bushings & bearings in Aerospace
