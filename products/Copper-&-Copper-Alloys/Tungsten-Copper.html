Tungsten Copper

Tungsten Copper Supplier in Singapore

Tungsten copper combines the high density and wear resistance of tungsten with the thermal and electrical conductivity of copper. It is used for electrodes, heat sinks and electrical contacts. Teck Leong Industries supplies tungsten copper bars, plates and rods in Singapore. Send us your grade and size for availability and a quote.
