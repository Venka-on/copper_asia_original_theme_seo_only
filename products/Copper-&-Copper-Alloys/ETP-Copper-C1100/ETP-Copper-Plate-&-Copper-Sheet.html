ETP Copper Plate & Copper Sheet


Send Enquiry   

ETP Copper Sheet C1100 & ETP Copper Plate  C1100 are used extensively for stamping, deep drawing, forming, polishing and machining. Their distinctive clear bright reddish brown colour makes them suitable for ornamental and architectural purposes. They are highly essential material used in automotive, electrical system, plumbing & fittings, marine engineering, telecommunications and many other applications.
