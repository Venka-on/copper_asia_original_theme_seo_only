EDM Copper

High-Conductivity EDM Copper for Precision Work

EDM Copper electrodes are high strength electrolytic copper. They undergo special advance processes to obtain optimum results for EDMing by providing high material removal rate and low wear rate. As every single electrode plate is ultra-sonic tested for porosity, its machining performance is guaranteed.
