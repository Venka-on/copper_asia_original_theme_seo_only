COPPER SHEET & COPPER PLATE
 
Copper Sheet C1100P is used extensively for stamping, deep drawing, forming, polishing and machining. Its distinctive clear bright reddish brown colour makes it suitable for ornamental and architectural purposes. It is highly essential material used in automotive, electrical system, plumbing & fittings, marine engineering, telecommunications and many other applications.
