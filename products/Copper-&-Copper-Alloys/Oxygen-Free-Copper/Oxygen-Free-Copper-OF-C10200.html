Oxygen-Free Copper C10200

 

Oxygen Free Copper C10200 is an ideal material for applications in which extremely high electrical conductivity and resistance to hydrogen embrittlement are vital. In application, where glass-to-metal seals are required, this material provides the added advantage of developing an adherent, non-scaling oxide at elevated temperatures. It is corrosion resistant in normal atmosphere and water. It achieves a minimum 100% IACS conductivity rating.

 

Oxygen Free Copper C10200 possesses useful qualities such as high ductility & high impact strength, good thermal conductivity, good creep resistance, low volatility in high vacuum condition and can be readily soldered and brazed.

 

RELEVANT SPECIFICATIONS

ASTM B187

 

 

APPLICATIONS

•  Electrical & Electronic Conductors

•  Superconductor matrixes
•  Lead-in Wires & Anodes for Vacuum Tubes
•  Vacuum Seals & Glass-to-Metal Seals
•  Power Substations
•  Coaxial cables
•  Microwave Tubes & Rectifiers
•  Plasma

 

TYPICAL CHEMICAL COMPOSITION

Minimum 99.95% Copper
