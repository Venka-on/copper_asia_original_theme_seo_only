Oxygen-Free Electronic Copper C10100
 
 
Oxygen-Free Electronic Copper C10100 is a 99.99% pure copper.  It achieves a minimum 101% IACS conductivity rating. It is classified as having 0.0005% oxygen content and is forged in an oxygen free environment. 
 
Oxygen-Free Electronic Copper C10100 has high ductility, high electrical and thermal conductivity and low volatility under high vacuum condition.  Its unique qualities of purity, conductivity, malleability and consistency make it the material of choice for many applications in the electrical, electronic, heat transfer and other industries.

 

Applications:

•   Casting Molds
•   Electric Motors
•   Automotive Rectifiers
•   Transistor Component Bases
•   Hollow Conductors
•   Coaxial Cables
•   Electronics
•   Backing Plates
•   High End Speaker 
 
 
Relevant Specifications
ASTM B187/B187M
 
 
Typical Chemical Composition
Minimum 99.99% Copper
