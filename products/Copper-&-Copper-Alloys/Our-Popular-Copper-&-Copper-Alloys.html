Our Popular Copper & Copper Alloys
 

Copper being corrosive resistant, recyclable, ductile and malleable, can easily be worked to requirements. The ease with which it can be drawn into wire makes it useful for electrical work in addition to its excellent electrical properties. It is used as a thermal conductor, an electrical conductor, copper busbars and a constituent of various metal alloys.

Copper has been utilized for many years in our daily life and are extensively used in many industrial and commercial applications as in communication devices, architectural designs, electricity and energy devices, plumbing and heating system, transportation system, solar energy photovoltaic cells and as a building material. Its durability and maintenance-free characteristics make copper the leading choice for almost any kind of applications. Copper is also a safe material in that it will not burn or support combustion and will not decompose into toxic gases.
