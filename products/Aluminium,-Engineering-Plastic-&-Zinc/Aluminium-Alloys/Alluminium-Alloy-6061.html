Aluminium Alloy 6061

Alloy 6061 has good corrosion resistance property and provides good anodising qualities for protection and surface hardening. Alloy 6061 is very suitable for machining on automatic lathes. Machinability for the harder T4 and T6 tempers is good. It can also be readily welded by common welding techniques. This alloy is commonly used where good corrosion resistance is essential, such as in components for marine industry, tank fittings, structural installation and high pressure applications, truck, pipelines and others.
