Aluminium Alloy 7075

Alloy 7075 is the strongest and hardest aluminium alloy. Machinability for this alloy is good and is best machined in annealed condition. Resistance welding is the only suitable method for joining this alloy. It is primarily used in aircraft, defence industry and highly stressed structural parts.
