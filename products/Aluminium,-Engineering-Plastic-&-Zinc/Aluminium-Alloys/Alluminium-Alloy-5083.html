Aluminium Alloy 5083

Alloy 5083 is suitable for marine applications as it has good corrosion resistance. This alloy has good strength and can be easily welded and machined by conventional methods. It is usually applied in the manufacture of transportation equipment, drilling rigs, some missile components, welded pressure vessels and others.
