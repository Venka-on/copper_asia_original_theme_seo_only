Alluminium Alloy 2024

Alloy 2024 is an age hardening strength aluminium alloy and is used when high mechanical properties are required. The machining property of this alloy is fair in the heat treated condition and is good when in the annealed condition. This alloy is used in the manufacturing of aircraft structures, scientific apparatus, rivets, automobile wheels and others.
