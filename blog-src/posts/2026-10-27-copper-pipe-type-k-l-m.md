Copper pipe is sold in three main types under the ASTM B88 standard: Type K, Type L and Type M. They look alike, but the wall thickness is different, and that affects pressure rating, durability and price. This guide explains the differences so you can specify the right pipe for your project.

## What ASTM B88 covers

ASTM B88 is the standard specification for seamless copper water tube. For a given nominal size, Types K, L and M all have the same outside diameter. Only the wall thickness changes, so the same fittings fit all three types.

## Type K, L and M at a glance

| Type | Wall thickness | Typical use |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Type K | Thickest | Underground service lines and demanding installations |
| Type L | Medium | General interior plumbing and many commercial projects |
| Type M | Thinnest | Lighter-duty domestic water lines where the specification allows it |

Always follow your consultant's specification and the requirements of the relevant authority. Typical uses vary by project and region.

## Wall thickness example: 1/2 inch nominal size

For a nominal 1/2 inch pipe, the outside diameter is 0.625 in (15.9 mm) for all three types. The wall thickness is:

- **Type K:** 0.049 in (about 1.25 mm)
- **Type L:** 0.040 in (about 1.02 mm)
- **Type M:** 0.028 in (about 0.71 mm)

Confirm these figures against the current ASTM B88 table and our available-sizes sheets before you order.

## How to choose the right type

- **Check the specification first.** The project drawings or your consultant usually name the type.
- **Think about the environment.** Buried, outdoor or high-wear runs often call for a thicker wall.
- **Balance cost and durability.** A thicker wall costs more per metre but gives a larger safety margin.
- **Match your fittings.** Because the outside diameter is the same, you can use [copper and cast copper alloy fittings](@products/Copper-and-Cast-Copper-Alloy-Fittings-&-Flanges) across all three types.
